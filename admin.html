📋 Referee — how to use this screen
  1. Pick your match below, then press ▶ Kick off. Press ⏭ End first half at the break, ▶ Resume for the second, and ⏭ End the match at full-time. That is all the clock needs.
  2. A goal is four taps. GOAL under the scoring team → tap who scored → tap where on the pitch → tap who assisted. The score goes up on the first tap, so if play restarts before you finish, just press Close — the goal is already saved. The last two questions can always be skipped.
  3. A save is one tap. Press SAVE under the defending team. It goes to that team's goalkeeper, whose name is printed on the button.
  4. A clearance is two taps. Press CLEARANCE, then the player who cleared it. Defenders are listed first.
  5. Made a mistake? Everything you log appears in the Match log at the bottom, newest first, with a Remove button. Removing a goal also takes it back off the scoreline. Nothing is ever stuck.

Log every save and clearance you see — they are what decide the Golden Glove and Best Defender medals. If you miss one, it simply is not counted; nothing breaks.
